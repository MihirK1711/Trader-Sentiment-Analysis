# Trader Performance vs Market Sentiment Analysis

## Objective
Analyze how market sentiment (Fear vs Greed) influences trader behavior and profitability.

---

## Methodology
- Data cleaning and preprocessing
- Timestamp conversion
- Dataset merging
- Feature engineering
- Sentiment-based analysis
- Trader segmentation

---

## Key Insights

1. Higher profitability observed during Greed sentiment.
2. Fear markets show higher volatility and risk.
3. Traders are more active during Greed periods.
4. Long positions dominate in Greed markets.

---

## Strategy Recommendations

- Reduce position size and risk during Fear markets
- Increase momentum-based trading during Greed periods
